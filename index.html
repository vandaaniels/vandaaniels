Private Bedrock Gameplay Systems R&D

Private development · Minecraft Bedrock
PRIVATE

A private research and development project exploring how complex Java-style magic and gameplay systems can be represented cleanly in Minecraft Bedrock without hard-coding every possible combination.

  • Dynamic spell execution architecture with ordered forms, effects and modifiers.
  • Persistent player-created configurations and multiplayer-safe execution.
  • Extensible TypeScript/JavaScript architecture designed to support additional mechanics over time.
  • Combination of Script API with native Bedrock pack systems instead of placing all logic in scripts.
